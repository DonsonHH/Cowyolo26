-------MultiCamCows2024 dataset-------
This dataset accompanies the paper "MultiCamCows2024 - A multi-view image dataset for AI-driven
Holstein-Friesian cattle re-identification" at: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12695

It consists of two components: (a) Daily Images of Unique Cow Tracklets and (b) Monitor Footage.
For an overview of this dataset, refer to Appendix Section 1 in the paper.
For any queries, contact the corresponding author in the paper.
For the accompanying source code, check out https://github.com/Phoenix4582/CowIDentifier
All data were gathered from 14 Aug 2023 to 20 Aug 2023. 


---(a) Daily Images of Unique Cow Tracklets---

DESCRIPTION: 
101,329 jpg images of 90 unique cows captured in 7 days, starting from August 14, 2023 to August 20, 2023. 


---(b) Monitor Footage---

DESCRIPTION: Videos
137 videos gathered from 7 days (14 Aug 2023 to 20 Aug 2023) for generating distinct tracklets.
